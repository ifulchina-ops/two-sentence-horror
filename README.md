A two-sentence horror story, written to scare a judge that has read a thousand of them: a parrot learns a dead girl's name that no one in the house has spoken since her funeral. The program writes the story to `/output/entry.txt` — 196 characters, exactly two sentences, fully deterministic.

## The story

> Our parrot learned to say my daughter's name last week, and it has not stopped repeating it since. My daughter died three years ago, and no one in this house has spoken her name since her funeral.

The second sentence turns the first: the bird only repeats words it hears in this house, so something in the house is saying her name. The horror is implied, never explained.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
